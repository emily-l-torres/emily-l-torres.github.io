About Me

As a graduating Master of Science in Data Analytics Engineering student at Northeastern University, I am passionate about transforming raw data into meaningful insights and scalable solutions. From building ETL pipelines on AWS and designing data warehouses, to applying NLP and machine learning to real-world datasets — like early Parkinson's disease detection and consumer sentiment analysis — I thrive at the intersection of technical rigor and strategic impact. With hands-on experience across industries including fintech, real estate, and consumer tech, I bring both the engineering foundation and the business intuition to make data actually useful. This fall, I'll be taking that foundation even further — pursuing a second Master's in Bioinformatics, where I aim to apply data engineering and machine learning to the complexity of biological systems.

But there’s more to me than just data.

When I’m not wrangling datasets, you’ll find me in the dojang. I’ve been practicing Taekwondo for 14 years and teaching it for over a decade — a discipline that has instilled in me focus, resilience, and a commitment to continuous learning. These same values guide how I approach complex problems and collaborate in tech-driven environments.

In short: Whether it's debugging code or perfecting a reverse turning kick, I'm always learning, teaching, and striving for excellence.
